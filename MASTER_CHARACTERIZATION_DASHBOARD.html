🚫 What Not To Do
Promote as measured lessons only when V8 evidence supports them
Do not choose topology from peak tokens/sec alone.
Do not call admission success production capacity.
Do not assume “fits in VRAM” means usable latency.
Do not assume prefill and decode prefer the same topology.
Do not extrapolate 128K behavior linearly to 1M.
Do not infer bandwidth sensitivity from a native-only campaign.
Do not infer NCCL/PP causality from E2E latency alone.
Do not compare unmatched workload conditions.
Do not use profiler-instrumented latency as normal benchmark latency.
Do not treat CUTLASS reference as model-kernel efficiency.
Do not tune scheduler knobs blindly if another subsystem dominates.
Do not render missing / unresolved evidence as zero.
