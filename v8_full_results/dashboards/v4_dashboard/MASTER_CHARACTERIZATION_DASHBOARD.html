WHY (Mechanism): The hybrid architecture combines 20 linear KDA layers with 7 full-attention layers. While KDA recurrence scales as O(N), full-attention layers scale as O(N²), causing attention to accumulate GPU work far faster as prompt length scales.

DECISION CHANGED: Do not tune long-context performance from a single 128K profile. Re-evaluate the optimization target as context increases because subsystem pressure scales at different rates. Prioritize FlashAttention & chunk budget for ≥512K; prioritize MoE routing and GEMMs for ≤128K.

WHAT happened: nvidia-smi / DCGM reports the percentage of time GPU kernels occupy compute queues. Polling spinning locks during cross-node AllReduce barriers registers as "active GPU", creating the illusion of high compute efficiency while serving 2.39× slower.
